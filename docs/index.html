How the Radar works

01

Search broadly

Daily queries include “seagrass”, eelgrass, marine angiosperm/phanerogam terminology and recognised seagrass genera, including historic taxonomic combinations.

02

Use independent sources

Crossref, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar and DOAJ reduce dependence on any single publisher or index.

03

Score + deduplicate

Records are scored for genuine seagrass relevance, false-positive-prone terms are checked, and duplicate DOI/title records are merged.

04

Preserve discovery date

“First seen” is retained separately from publication date, so a paper that appears late in an index still surfaces as newly discovered.

What “all journals” can realistically mean

No public scholarly index can guarantee instantaneous discovery of every paper on Earth. This Radar therefore maximises recall through overlapping indexes and exposes source/publisher health instead of pretending coverage is perfect. It does not bypass paywalls and does not scrape copyrighted full text.
